# Searchscape v2.1.2

Make private-tab setup easier to find: a Private-tab settings shortcut in the toolbar popup opens and focuses the setting directly. The private-tab card is now near the top of Customize Searchscape and includes a button to open this extension’s own browser Details page. The homepage Customize button reopens settings at the top.

The optional permission and automatic-private-tab behavior are unchanged. Allow in Private must still be enabled manually in Brave, and the optional tab-address permission must be granted after turning on the setting.

Reload the extension, click its toolbar icon and choose Private-tab settings.
