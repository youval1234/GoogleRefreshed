# GoogleRefreshed v1.5.1

Fixes the Rose theme: overlapping orbital loops are replaced with a layered rose bloom, shaded petals, defined leaves and leaf veins. The flower and stem now share one gentle sway, with a small bloom-opening animation.

Light/dark palettes, animation off, reduced motion, and the narrow layout were checked in an isolated Brave profile. The updated dark and light previews are included in docs/v15. No new permissions or dependencies.

Replace the files in the existing extension folder, reload GoogleRefreshed at brave://extensions, then refresh Google. The local preview only needs a refresh.
