# GoogleRefreshed v1.5.0

All themes have rebuilt scenery and stronger, measured motion. Fire and Magma bring the collection to 38 themes, with light, dark and system appearance.

- Aurora has layered northern-light curtains; Sakura has two blossom trees and independently drifting petals. Magical has a floating spellbook, orbiting seal and luminous orb. Midnight has a moonlit mountain lake and occasional meteors.
- Code, Math, Colorful and Rainbow have new compositions: editor and flowing circuit data, traced graphs and geometry, moving Memphis shapes, and prism light with a spectrum arc. Every other theme has its own scenery and animation pass.
- Animations stay behind the interface with a quiet center, Vivid/Subtle intensity, an off switch, reduced-motion support and hidden-tab pause. Only the selected scene is mounted. Blossom shapes are reused and skyline window animations are bounded.
- Up to 60 shortcuts, wrapping onto additional rows. An Add shortcut button beside the group selector and a visible count make expansion easier.
- The hardware card now keeps CPU/RAM model information and aligned usage graphs. The secondary GPU/network/unavailable-temperature block and its WebGL/connection probes are removed.
- Reset settings to default is near the top of Customize. A second click restores homepage preferences and default shortcuts, clears local personalization, resets the focus timer and turns off optional browser settings sync. Closing Customize cancels the pending confirmation. It does not uninstall a separately installed toolbar theme.
- Updated matching native toolbar themes: 76 light/dark folders, including Fire and Magma. They remain separate, manually installed companion themes. No new permissions are requested.

Validation: 153 controlled Brave checks covering native integration, all 38 themes in both appearances, real animation changes, motion controls, responsive layouts, more than six shortcuts, 60-link persistence, reset behavior, search engines, news, privacy boundaries and sync behavior. Source/history/archive privacy scan and dependency audit are included. Physical cross-device sync delivery and live Google AI pages are not asserted.

To update, replace the files in the existing extension folder, reload GoogleRefreshed at brave://extensions, and refresh Google. To remove an installed toolbar theme, use brave://settings/appearance → Theme → Reset to default.
