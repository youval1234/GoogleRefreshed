# GoogleRefreshed v1.4.0

A larger visual refresh with original scenery, more useful widgets, search-engine choice, and fixes for native Google controls and AI text.

- 36 distinct vector scenes: 19 new worlds include Storm, Nature, Sakura, City Skyline, Rainbow, Lunar, Math, Library, Office, and Science. Theme-specific movement replaces the common glow backdrop; rain, snow, leaves, petals, and embers use independent positions and timing. Storm has cloud banks, layered rain, and distant lightning.
- A world clock and locally persistent focus timer fill the clock card. Both are customizable.
- 12 fonts, including classic Google-style Arial and the original Georgia homepage font. Seven font families are bundled locally with their licenses.
- Google, DuckDuckGo, Brave Search, Bing, and Ecosia choices with original icons and a properly aligned chevron. Web, image, and video queries use your selection. Non-Google selection stops Google autocomplete prefixes. Maps and Lens continue to use Google.
- Themed dropdowns, upload buttons, and light/dark theme tiles. Rapid preference changes no longer revert because of older local save events.
- CPU, RAM, GPU, network, and thermometer icons; cleaner GPU names with device IDs in tooltips. Sampling interruptions retain useful readings and recover with a truthful status. Real CPU/GPU temperatures remain unavailable in Brave on Windows without a native companion.
- Safer Google AI styling preserves shared color-variable definitions and repairs low-contrast response text. Inline panels, gradients, and late updates follow the selected palette.
- A toolbar preview and clearer manual companion-theme installation, with 72 light/dark folders. Chrome/Brave requires this separate native theme; switching the page palette cannot install it automatically.

Validation: 142 controlled Brave checks; dependency audit and source/history/archive privacy scan. The hardware/private-frame and Google autocomplete/weather/news flows also have live smoke checks. Google search-result and AI tests use controlled fixtures; a live automated Google search may encounter a challenge, which is left untouched. PC-to-laptop sync delivery is not asserted.

Reload GoogleRefreshed at brave://extensions and refresh Google. For a downloaded copy, first replace the files in its existing folder. No new permissions are required. For toolbar colors, load the matching folder from the separate browser-themes ZIP.
